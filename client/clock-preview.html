RumiNation

The day clock and the Rumi, as pixel art

Redrawn pixel by pixel to match your characters: the same dark plum outline, flat two-tone colours and no smoothing. Noon is at the top and midnight at the bottom. The sun rises over the hills at 06:00 and sets at 18:00, then the moon, the stars and the little house's window come out. Drag the slider or play a day in 24 seconds.

The Rumi

A 16 × 16 grey-green stone coin with a square hole, like an old Chinese coin. Four small carved marks sit around the hole instead of real characters.

What to tell me

  • Is the clock right, or should anything change: colours, size, more or less gold, the hills and house?
  • The coin: keep the four abstract marks, or try real characters such as 通寶 ("circulating treasure", common on old coins)? Real characters would need a bigger coin, around 24 px, to stay readable.
