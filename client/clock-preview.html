The day clock and the Rumi coin

The sun and moon ride an arm around a carved stone ring. Noon is at the top and midnight at the bottom. The sun comes up over the hills on the left at 06:00 and sets on the right at 18:00, and at night the moon takes its place. Each clock sits on a patch of in-game grass so you can judge it where it will be. Drag the slider or press play to run a whole day in 24 seconds.

The Rumi

A round grey-green stone with a square hole, like an old Chinese coin. Instead of real characters, four short carved marks sit around the hole, so it reads as a coin without pretending to say something.

What to tell me

  • Smooth gold (A) or pixel gold (B)? Pixel matches the characters more closely; smooth looks grander.
  • Anything about the clock: colours, the hills, the size, more or less ornament.
  • For the coin: keep the abstract marks, or use real characters, such as 通寶 ("circulating treasure", common on old coins)?
